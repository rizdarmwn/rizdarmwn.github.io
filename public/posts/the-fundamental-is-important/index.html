The Fundamental Is Important

  • #yapping

I was reading a comic about martial arts where the protagonist is a martial artist reincarnated to the future. This protagonist, I call him now Boy, was one of the greatest martial artist, and his sect is also one. The reputation, land, wealth, people are their assets; yet what makes them on the top is the method.

Boy reincarnated to a little young boy, searching for his sect. Asked the people around him, and behold it fell off.

Boy went to the sect location, seeking to be their disciple. He got assigned as a newbie. The outer disciple. The first level to climb.

Boy went to training, the next day. Saw all of the outer disciples training. Keep in mind, they were also beginners. What they train was supposedly fundamentals. Fundamentals to go to the next level. Yet what he saw was something broken. The training focused on learning the martial art. When they were a beginner. No strength, no understanding, nothing.

Boy was complaining, why they were training this way. They were training a martial art that they have no prerequisites trained yet. What happens then?

Sloppiness. Weakness. Ignorance.

Why would they learn martial arts without strength?

Why would they build a house without a foundation?

Why would they write without understanding a single word?

Why would they code without understanding what is happening?

Why stop at being mediocre when they can be good?

Why stop at being good when they can be great?
